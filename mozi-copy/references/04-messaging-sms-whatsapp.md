# 04 — Messaging: SMS / WhatsApp / DM Copy

Every text-message script in the source material, with timing and mechanism. The cadence system that decides *when* these fire is in `02-outreach-and-nurture.md`.

---

## Messaging principles

- **Automated reminders remind; they don't make people care.** Have automations as a safety net, but the messages that move show rates are visibly personal: the prospect's name, your face, your voice, a joke. "We're using systematization instead of automation to accomplish the same goal." *(gym-launch/08)*
- **Look human.** iMessage/WhatsApp over SMS-blast green bubbles where possible ("blue messages") — people still don't fully believe a real person is texting. Voice notes and short personal videos crush for exactly this reason. *(yt/01)*
- **Photos are tonality.** A goofy selfie, a thumbs-up, a picture pointing at the front door — these carry the energy a phone call would. Smileys do the same in text.
- **Every message: one goal, one specific time, scarcity or urgency on the CTA.** "Text me right now," "tonight," "only a few spots."
- **Always confirm.** An unconfirmed booking is a coin flip at best. Treat unconfirmed scheduled leads like unscheduled leads. *(gym-launch/01)*

## Confirmation scripts (right after they book — "hot leads")

> **Verbatim:** "Hey, this is Taylor — your assigned six-week challenge coach. I saw you registered for an orientation with me. You need to text me right now to confirm your appointment time — we only have 8 spots left."
> → "Yeah, I want to do it." → "Perfect. See you then."

Then at noon before the appointment, a goofy thumbs-up selfie: *"Look out for this guy at the gym when you come in — see you soon!"* *(gym-launch/09)*

**Generalized:** "Hey, this is [name] — your [role] for [program]. I saw you booked [thing] with me. Text me back right now to confirm your spot — we've only got [n] left."

**Mechanism:** assigning yourself to them ("your coach") creates a person to not let down; "text me right now" is a micro-commitment; the spot count is scarcity. The noon selfie converts an appointment into meeting a specific human.

## Didn't book ("warm leads")

> **Verbatim:** "Hey, this is Taylor. We noticed you weren't able to schedule — if you're still interested in the challenge you need to text me right now, because we only have a handful of spots left."
> → "Yes, I'm interested." → "Awesome — what time can you drop by for 15 minutes between 3 and 8pm today? …5pm? Perfect, I'll put you in my calendar." Then the noon selfie text as above. *(gym-launch/09)*

**Mechanism:** assumes interest survived (it did — life got in the way), demands a tiny instant action, offers a *window* then locks a specific time, shrinks the commitment ("15 minutes", "drop by").

## The reminder sequence (booked → showed)

Same-day/next-day bookings need exactly two reminder windows; here's all three for longer gaps:

**1. Night before (send 8–9pm — they're planning tomorrow; daytime reminders get forgotten, evening ones get woken up with):**
- *Elite version — personal video:* "Hey Sally, it's Alex! Just wanted to drop you a line — you're coming in tomorrow at [time] as my personal guest. I hope you haven't forgotten about us, because I have not forgotten about you. Looking forward to meeting you — it's going to be awesome. See you then!"
- *Minimum version — text:* "Friendly reminder about your [program name] appointment tomorrow at 4pm with Alex (that's me 🙂). [Address]. See you then!"
- *B2B version (shows work already done):* "Hey John, really excited to meet tomorrow. We outlined six things for your business I think will really help — pulled them from my team and from looking over your site before we get on the call. So stoked. We're at 4 o'clock — hit me up if any issues getting on, otherwise see you then." *(gym-launch/08, yt/01)*

**2. Morning of:** call first; if no pickup, personal video/voice note: "Hey Susie — stoked to see you tonight! Reminder you don't need to bring workout stuff, this is a no-sweat, just-get-to-know-you session — figure out your goals and how we can help. Oh — text me back your shirt size so I can pull one aside for you. See you tonight at [time]!"
**Mechanism:** removes friction/fear ("no sweat session"), and the shirt-size question demands a reply *and* plants a gift waiting for them — they now feel worse about not showing. The generalized form is the **preference question**: *"I set aside a little something for everyone coming in this week — would you rather an Amazon card, Starbucks, or [X]?"* Any answered either/or before the appointment works: they've invested, reciprocity is loaded ("this guy's doing this thing — least I can do is show up"). B2B equivalent: "I set aside [the deliverable/finding] for you." 60 minutes of these a day ≈ +30% show rate. *(gym-launch/08, yt/01, yt/03)*

**3. ~1 hour before** (batch them: if appointments run 4–8pm, send all between 3–4): "Sally — heads up, we're the building across from [landmark]. If you have any trouble finding us, give me a holler — I'll come out and get you." Attach a photo of you pointing at the front door, making a dumb face. *(gym-launch/08)*

## Last-push / re-engagement copy

- **The last push** (day ~10, or final ~14 days of a promotion — "always pays for itself"): light, no-guilt re-open + scarcity: *"Hey [name] — must have missed you! Maybe life got in the way. We're closing up [program] spots this week — if you still want in, just shoot me a message."* *(gym-launch/01, 03)*
- **The meme revival:** to a cold pipeline, just send the "where you at??" meme (the Kevin Hart one). Highest response rate of any message tested; 200 sends reliably re-engage 5–10 laughing prospects. *(yt/01)*

## Post-sale messages (keep the sale closed)

Right after the sale, **reinforce** — especially from someone other than the closer:
> "Hey [name], it's [owner name] — I own [business]. So great to have you! I see you're starting Saturday at 9 — everyone loves [the first step], you're going to do great. Anything you need, text me here."

"Just give them a little hug. It's going above and beyond, and they will appreciate it more than I can tell you." Buyer's remorse is killed with proof and warmth in the first 24 hours, not silence. *(gym-launch/27)*

## Channel adaptation quick notes

- **WhatsApp:** identical to SMS scripts; voice notes replace personal videos 1:1; the noon selfie works verbatim.
- **Instagram/LinkedIn DM:** lead with the recognition anchor ("you [liked/commented/downloaded] X") exactly like the phone opener's source-callback; keep the assigned-human frame ("I'm the one who handles everyone who grabs that guide").
- **Email:** these scripts compress into subject + 2 lines + one CTA; see `10-channel-adaptation.md`.
