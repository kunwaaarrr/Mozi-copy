# 10 — Channel Adaptation: Applying the System Anywhere

The source material is phone + SMS + in-person. This file maps it onto every channel the user actually writes for. Rule: **the psychology never changes; only the packaging does.** Identify the funnel stage, pull the matching reference, then apply the channel rules below.

## Channel rules

### WhatsApp / SMS / iMessage
The native habitat — scripts in `04` work nearly verbatim.
- One message = one goal = one question. End on the question.
- Specific times, not "sometime this week." Offer a window, lock a slot.
- Voice note = the personal video. Selfie/photo = tonality. One emoji where the grin goes.
- Scarcity quantified ("only 3 spots", "I close the list tonight"), urgency dated ("by Friday").
- Night-before sends at 8–9pm; batch hour-before sends.
- Re-engage with the meme before you re-engage with a pitch.

### Email
Compression of the same beats:
- **Subject = the recognition anchor or the curiosity gap**, never the pitch ("about your [thing they did]", "you left this open", "must have missed you").
- **First line = acknowledge + associate** (3A works in writing): "Saw you grabbed the guide — smart move, it's the same first step our best clients took."
- **Body = pain cycle compressed**: where you are → where you want to be → the one missing pillar → the bridge. Three pillars max, one metaphor, specific numbers over adjectives.
- **One CTA**, low-bar phrasing ("worth a look?" / "want the spot?" — the written "sound fair?"), with scarcity/urgency attached.
- **PS = the second close** — a foil story, a testimonial line, or the deadline restated. The PS is the most-read line after the subject.
- Cold email = the cold-outreach research play in `03`: lead with the specific observed problem and the pre-worked solution; never open with yourself.

### DMs (Instagram / LinkedIn / X)
- Open with the *source callback* exactly like the phone opener: "you commented on / downloaded / liked X."
- Assigned-human frame: "I'm the one who works with everyone who grabs that."
- Short volleys — DM threads are loops (`06` §looping): resolve one concern per message, end each with a question.
- Research opener for cold DMs (the gym-owner profile-piecing play in `03`).

### Sales pages / VSLs / webinars (one-to-many)
The sales conversation (`05`) monologued — you must *play both sides*:
- Ask the questions and voice their answers ("You've probably tried X. And it worked — until it didn't. Here's what was missing…").
- Kill zombies in the body copy (obstacles before price — Law 6): spouse, time, "I've been burned before" each get their section *before* the price reveal.
- Stack proof environmentally like the testimonial walls (`09`): interleaved, overwhelming, throughout — not one quarantined logos row.
- Price like the menu: anchor tier first, recommend the middle, cascade the terms.
- Guarantee phrased with conditions ("provided you…") then best-case/worst-case close.
- The 1-to-10, unicorn, and consider-the-options closes all convert to rhetorical sequences near the CTA.

### Proposals / quotes / B2B docs
- Frame as investment with return data, never cost (`07` §5).
- MESOs: three options, all acceptable to you; the recommendation marked "what most clients do" (the written "what most people do is prepay — fair enough?").
- Anchor high tier first. Terms move, price doesn't.
- BAMFAM in writing: the proposal names the next meeting date before they've accepted.

### Enterprise / long-cycle B2B (committees, 6–12 month cycles)

Most of the one-call playbook **does not apply** here — no same-day close, no cash cascade. What transfers, and what changes: *(yt/01)*
- **Stay in the pocket**: the job is relationship maintenance across the whole cycle — BAMFAM every contact, notes-driven follow-ups (`02`), the 3–6-month pulse rhythm compressed to weeks.
- **Bond in webs, not threads**: one champion ↔ one rep is a single thread that snaps when either leaves. Connect *multiple* people on both sides (your engineer ↔ their engineer, your CS ↔ their ops) — a network of bonds is what makes the account unbreakable, for them and for you.
- **Pre-education is the close** (Law 14 at maximum): committees aren't overcome on calls; they're educated between them — case studies, proof, internal-champion ammunition. Write copy your champion can forward.
- Obstacles-before-price still rules: surface every stakeholder's zombie (procurement, security, the CFO) in discovery, not at signature.

### "General conversations" (any persuasion: spouse, boss, landlord)
- 3A everything (`07`).
- Questions over statements — they believe what they say (Law 3).
- The decision taxonomy (`06`) works on yourself too: notice whether you/they are casting power to circumstances, others, or self, and apply the matching close gently.
- Support-not-permission, best-case/worst-case, and closer-or-further are life closes, not just sales closes.

## Industry translation (de-fitness-ifying any script)

Every fitness-specific element maps mechanically:
| Fitness original | The slot it fills | Examples elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Lose 30 lbs / goal weight | The prize (quantified end state) | Revenue target, placement rate, close rate, downloads |
| 6-week challenge / 42-day detox | The front-end promotion (irrelevant wrapper — Law 4) | Audit, trial, pilot, workshop |
| Fitness / nutrition / accountability | The three pillars | (Re-derive: "what 3 things does an amazing [outcome] require?") |
| Weigh-in / before-after photos | The measurable check-in artifact | Dashboards, weekly reports, KPI reviews |
| "I'm here till 8" | The availability fact that unlocks booking | "I have two slots tomorrow," "I'm in your area Thursday" |
| Supplements ("take what I tell you to take") | The recurring attach that funds the guarantee | Retainers, maintenance plans, licenses |
| The gym tour | The demo environment (`09` staging) | Office visit, dashboard walkthrough, portfolio page |

**The four-part pattern for any borrowed script:** (1) keep the *mechanism* (what psychological lever it pulls), (2) swap the prize and pillars for the new industry, (3) keep the *rhythm* — the recap-back, the "fair enough?", the steamroll, the tone-switch all survive translation, (4) re-check against `08`'s written-voice rules and `01`'s law checklist.
